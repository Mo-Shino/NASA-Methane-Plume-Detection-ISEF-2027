## 📏 ISEF Display Board Dimensions & Standards
* **Max Width:** 122 cm (48 in)
* **Max Depth:** 76 cm (30 in)
* **Max Height:** 274 cm (108 in) from floor / 165 cm (65 in) on table
* **Compliance Note:** No student names, mentor names, school affiliations, or commercial endorsements appear on the board, ensuring 100% compliance with Regeneron ISEF Rule 2.
